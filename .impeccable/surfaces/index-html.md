# Surface: Fundo Admin landing (front door, before sign-in)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: existing Bayanihan MPC staff (admin, credit manager, membership officer) arriving signed out. Action: Sign in (leads to the prototype account picker). Proof: the product's own credit-check output on seeded sample data, labelled sample. No customers, figures or pricing claims. Dashboard look is unchanged; the page lives in the same world (lime #CDEB5B, ink #191C15, system type).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Fundo Admin explains itself. The headline is a credit check exactly as Fundo writes it, with its peso figures highlighted. Refuses the category default of headline + subcopy + screenshot + feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, ink type, lime used only as the highlighter under figures and the logo tile. A 2px ink rule anchors indexes. Ruled rows, tabular figures, system sans at display size, tight tracking. No cards, no gradients, no icon tiles.

STORY: Staff see a real-looking check sentence, understand that applications arrive pre-checked against their co-op's rules, see what each role can do, and sign in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the Fundo mark left and Sign in right. Applicant line (name, loan, amount, term). A huge sentence (~7vw) with lime-marked figures. Under it, a one-line explanation and a primary Sign in button. The five checks are pinned along the bottom as a ruled index, and "Next application" re-runs the sentence on another sample application.
